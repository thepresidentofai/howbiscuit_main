How Biscuit is a general-purpose explainer site for clear, answer-first articles across practical topics.

The site is organized into six public divisions: Math, Research & Writing, Cooking, Home Tech, Make-Do Lab, and Tools. The current build establishes the publishing shell and preserves the first two article routes while future articles are developed separately.
